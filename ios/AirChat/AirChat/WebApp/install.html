📱 iPhone no App Store needed

AirChat for iOS is not on the App Store: a self-hosted relay + the background keep-alive trick would not survive Apple's review. You do not need the $99 developer account either — a free Apple ID can install it.

  1. Get the Sideloadly (Windows/Mac) or AltServer/SideStore tool once.
  2. Grab AirChat.ipa from this server: /download-app/AirChat-unsigned.ipa (or from the GitHub Actions artifact of the repo).
  3. Drop the .ipa on the tool, sign with your own Apple ID, install. Apple allows 3 sideloaded apps per free account; the certificate lasts 7 days and AltStore/SideStore refresh it for you.

Already have a paid developer account? Then use Xcode → Product ▸ Archive → Distribute to TestFlight (see ios/README.md).

💻 Laptop, tablet, anything with a browser

Nothing to install. Open the address in your browser's bar — the room key is delivered inside the link and never touches the server.

Voice notes need a secure origin (https or localhost), so from a laptop browser use text, images and BUZZ. Both native apps record natively and hand the audio to the chat, so voice notes work on Android and on iPhone.

⚠️ If the host is an iPhone

iOS suspends backgrounded apps. Keep the AirChat app open, or turn on Keep serving when locked in its Server panel (Settings gear ▸ Background), and don't force-quit it. Everyone must stay on the same Wi-Fi / hotspot.
